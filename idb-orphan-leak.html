IndexedDB Orphaned Data Leak

Generating massive "ghost" data by race-conditions during database deletion

This repro simulates a race condition where high-frequency database creation and writes followed by periodic deletions cause LevelDBScopes to leak orphaned data on disk.

To trigger the leak: Click "Start Leaking". Every minute, the script will pause, delete all databases it created, and wait 10 seconds for the browser's quota accounting to settle. The residual usage (the "amount leaked") will be logged below.
