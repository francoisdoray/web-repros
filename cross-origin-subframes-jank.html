Cross-Origin Subframes Jank

Main frame jank and process storm caused by injecting 50 cross-site subframes all at once.

Why does this cause jank?

Each subdomain of github.io is a distinct eTLD+1 because github.io is listed on the Public Suffix List (PSL).

  • Under Chromium's Site Isolation, each distinct site triggers the creation of an Out-Of-Process Iframe (OOPIF) and allocation of renderer processes.
  • Injecting 50 frames synchronously floods the main frame renderer and browser process with FrameTreeNode, RenderFrameProxyHost, and Mojo IPC setup.
